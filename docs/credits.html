Credits & Foundations

Elite was written by Ian Bell and David Braben. Commodore 64 Elite is copyright © D. Braben and I. Bell, 1985.

Alongside my own modifications, Elite: Unbound builds on Mark Moxon’s documented Commodore 64 source code and flicker-free work. The player-ship concept and data model are inspired by Angus Duggan’s Elite-A, with additional ideas drawn from Uz’s Elite 128 for the C64. Elite: Unbound is maintained by Ataribaby.
